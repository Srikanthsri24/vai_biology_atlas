This release includes detailed anatomical surface meshes, rather than primitive mannequin geometry, in the body, muscular, skeletal, brain and selected organ modules. It does not reproduce the proprietary models in reference screenshots.

What is—and is not—included

The body combines independently authored surface datasets registered using shared femur landmarks. It is a teaching composite, not a patient scan or clinically validated registration. Surface colors are illustrative; photographic skin textures, tissue microstructure, rigged joint mechanics, and clinical volumetric data are not included.

Coverage varies: the nervous module includes detailed brain regions but not a complete peripheral nerve network. The cardiovascular module includes heart and pulmonary vessels, not the complete systemic vasculature. The skeleton contains 201 source meshes, which is not a claim that all 206 canonical bones are individually represented. The male body and male reproductive module use male-source geometry.

Female anatomy, female reproductive anatomy, kidneys, urinary system, eye, ear, skin cross section and microscopic studies remain clearly labelled schematic development models. Kidney meshes from the Z-Anatomy export were excluded because its source notices identify a noncommercial kidney asset. No inner-ear asset from that export was included.

Living Organs animations are conceptual teaching illustrations; they do not calculate physiological pressures, fluid flow, neural voltage or filtration. Cross sections clip surfaces and do not reconstruct tissue interiors.
